Equal Air Collaborative.

A cross-hub project on the health, environmental, and economic costs of air pollution — and what cleaner air could mean for our cities.

The problem

Outdoor air pollution kills an estimated 4.2 million people worldwide every year. There is increasing global recognition of the damage poor air quality does to health, the environment, and the economy — but action hasn't kept pace with awareness.

What we're building

Run in partnership with the Global Future Council, the Equal Air Initiative has already convened 20 thought leaders and 35 Global Future Council members.

  • 01

    Raise awareness

    Of the health, environmental, and economic impacts of air pollution.

  • 02

    Promote solutions

    Supporting solutions that improve air quality in urban communities.

  • 03

    Drive collective action

    Bringing together communities, organizations, and leaders to advance healthier, more sustainable cities.
