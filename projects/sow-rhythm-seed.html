Active project — Gardening & Culture

Sow Rhythm Seed.

Hands-on gardening and culture workshops that root Brooklyn's next generation in history, community, and care for the land. Led by Cherise.

Overview

Sow Rhythm Seed combines gardening, culture, and community through hands-on workshops. Sessions celebrate the legacy of Louis & Lucille Armstrong while exploring local Brooklyn history — teaching participants how to mix soil, plant seeds, and care for what they grow.

What participants get

  • 01

    Hands-on planting

    Real soil, real seeds — participants learn the basics of planting and plant care in a supportive group setting.

  • 02

    Local history & culture

    Workshops are woven with the legacy of Louis & Lucille Armstrong and Brooklyn's own community history.

  • 03

    Take-home gardening kits

    Every participant leaves with a kit to keep growing at home — inspiring greener communities beyond the workshop.
