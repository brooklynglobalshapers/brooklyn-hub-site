Climate Connect.

Bringing Brooklyn together around climate action through conversation, storytelling, and art. Led by Pranathi.

Overview

Climate Connect brings people together to connect around climate action and share ideas for a more sustainable Brooklyn — empowering young leaders through conversations, storytelling, and a collaborative art activity, and inspiring community action by highlighting local solutions.

Objectives

  • 01

    Amplify local solutions

    Surface and spread Brooklyn-based solutions to climate challenges.

  • 02

    Foster dialogue

    Create space for community-led conversation and connection around climate.

  • 03

    Empower youth leaders

    Center young people and underrepresented voices as climate leaders.

  • 04

    Arts-based action

    Inspire collaborative, arts-based climate action with lasting impact.

Who it's for

Young people ages 16–35, local artists, educators, and community organizers; frontline climate-impacted communities in Brooklyn; and partners and supporters interested in grassroots climate action.

Want to lead this?

Climate Connect is looking for a new lead this term. If this is your area, we'd love to talk.
