Active project — Youth Collective Initiative

Link the Lunch.

Getting fresh, nutritious meals to community fridges where they're needed most. Led by Ky'Naisha & Vanshika.

Overview

Link the Lunch partners with Brooklyn's community fridges to distribute meals directly to neighbors who need them. It's part of our Youth Collective Initiative, built around three goals: reducing food waste, increasing access to fresh food, and strengthening community care through shared meals and mutual aid.

What we focus on

  • 01

    Reducing food waste

    Rescuing meals and groceries that would otherwise go unused and getting them to people instead.

  • 02

    Access to fresh food

    Stocking community fridges across Brooklyn with nutritious options, not just shelf-stable goods.

  • 03

    Mutual aid

    Building community care through shared meals — neighbors looking out for neighbors.
